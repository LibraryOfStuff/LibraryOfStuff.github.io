The Beginning

Skyward Sword

Skyward Sword takes place near the beginning of the Zelda timeline. Link lives in Skyloft and eventually discovers the land below.

The Minish Cap

Link meets the Picori and has to stop Vaati from causing trouble in Hyrule.

Four Swords

Link uses the Four Sword to split into four different Links and fight Vaati.

Ocarina of Time

Link travels through time and fights Ganondorf. This is where the timeline splits into different paths.

The Timeline Splits

After Ocarina of Time, the timeline continues in different directions.

Child Timeline

Majora's Mask

Link travels to Termina and becomes involved in the strange events surrounding the moon.

Twilight Princess

Link becomes involved with the Twilight Realm and has to save Hyrule.

Four Swords Adventures

Link once again has to deal with Vaati and another dangerous threat.

Adult Timeline

The Wind Waker

Hyrule has been flooded and most of the world is now covered by the Great Sea.

Phantom Hourglass

Link continues his journey across the Great Sea.

Spirit Tracks

A new Hyrule is established in a new land.

Fallen Hero Timeline

A Link to the Past

Link travels between the Light World and the Dark World to save Hyrule.

Link's Awakening

Link wakes up on the mysterious island of Koholint.

A Link Between Worlds

Link discovers the parallel world of Lorule.
